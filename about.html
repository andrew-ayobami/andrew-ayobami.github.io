About ArmsWatch

ArmsWatch is an ongoing effort to track notable developments in military applications of artificial intelligence, as rising AI capabilities become more evident and alluring to state actors.

Over time, I intend to cover as many countries as are relevant to the topic. That the current scope tracks only the United States, China, and the United Kingdom is merely because this is a work in progress. I also intend to cover the policy and risk implications of what these countries are doing.

I'm hosting it publicly before full completion in the hope that anyone interested in military applications of AI or worried about the growing bromance between governments and AI corps can see the direction and decide to contribute. If this is you, please reach out.
